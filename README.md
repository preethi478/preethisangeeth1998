THE WORKFORCE AT A GLANCE 

 The workforce at a glance presents an interactive HR analytics dashboard designed to analyze employee demographic, education, job roles,
work experience, hiring trends, salary distribution, and workforce location.
- PROBLEM DOMAIN:
Organizations need to understand their workforce to make effective HR decision. Employee data can be difficult to analyze when information about demographic, salaries, education, experience, and hiring trends is viewed separately.
- PROJECT OBJECTVES:
Analyze employee demographic, including age and education.
Evaluate average salary and employee headcount across department.
Identify yearly employee hiring trends.
Compare work experience across department.
Explore workforce distribution by location and job title.
Use interactive slicers and drill-down features to explore employee data.
Support data-driven HR decision-making through clear visualization.
- KEY DASHBOARD FEATURES:
Employee demographic and age-group analusis.
Department-wise headcount and average salary table.
Hiring trends by year.
Education and marital status distribution.
Work experience analysis by department.
Workforce distribution by location and job title.
Interactive slicers and drill-down analysis.
- TOOLS AND TECHNOLOGIES:
Microsoft power bi
Power query for data preparation
Dax for calculations and analysis
Interactive charts, tables, and maps, ai visualization.
- CONCLUSION:
This project transforms employee data into meaningful visual insights. It helps users understand workforce patterns, evaluate salary distribution, and explore HR information to support informed organizational decision.
